The brownstone

Up the stoop, second floor.

SOL NYC is a sunny 700-square-foot suite on the second floor of a private residential brownstone, on a tree-lined block in historic Bedford-Stuyvesant. High ceilings, hardwood floors throughout and charming details, set up for couples, solo travelers, business trips and families.

Hosted by Dmitri and Lea. Dmitri is an Airbnb Superhost with ten years of hosting.

The suite

Four rooms, all yours.

01 · Living room

A sofa, two lounge chairs and the second queen bed, with a 65-inch TV carrying premium cable and the streaming apps.

02 · Kitchen

Full-size stainless appliances, a gas range with a convection oven, a dishwasher and stone counters. Coffee and tea are stocked.

03 · Bedroom

A queen bed between tall windows, with a laptop desk and a printer for work days.

04 · Bath

A full bathroom with a tub and shower, a hair dryer and extra towels and linens.

Also yours: a terrace, a washer and dryer, wifi, an iron and a blender, toaster oven, microwave, coffee maker and kettle.

Good to know: the suite is private; the building entrance and common areas are shared. No smoking, no parties or events, registered guests only.
