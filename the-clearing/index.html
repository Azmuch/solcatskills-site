The Clearing at SOL

The Clearing.

A secluded gathering space surrounded by Catskills beauty.

A deck in the woods

Perfect for yoga, meditation, intimate acoustic concerts and meaningful connection.

Across the meadow and through the old fieldstone wall, a level deck sits in a stand of tall birch and maple. Sun comes through the canopy in patches.

The walk in

Through the old stone wall.

Out across the meadow, over the fieldstone wall at the tree line, then along a dirt path under the birches and maples until the deck appears. Look up when you get there.

What it’s for

No schedule yet. It’s yours to shape.

We don’t run classes or concerts at The Clearing yet. Here is what it was made for. The pictures of people below are illustrations, not past events.

Yoga & meditation

Morning practice on level boards under the canopy. An instructor can be arranged on request through independent local providers, booked separately.

Acoustic evenings

Intimate, unplugged sets for your group, with the forest for walls.

Meaningful connection

A circle, a toast, an hour with no phones. Whatever brought your group up here, give it a quiet place to happen.
