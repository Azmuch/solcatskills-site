The house

Space to spread out.

Four bedrooms, four baths and shared spaces big enough for twelve — with a sauna, a hot tub and a wood stove built into the day.

12guests
4bedrooms
7beds · 3 king, 4 full
4full baths
Saunacedar, indoor
Hot tubvalley-facing
01 · Main floor

One open room for cooking, eating and doing nothing.

The great room rises two stories with walls of sliding glass onto the deck. The kitchen's waterfall island faces the table, so whoever's cooking stays in the conversation.

02 · Lower Level

A second living space for the night owls.

The Lower Level opens through its own patio doors to the back of the property: a pool table, a game table, a wet bar with a wine fridge, a lounge with big-window views — and its own bedroom.

03 · Bedrooms & loft

Four bedrooms, an open loft, seven beds.

3 king beds and 4 full beds, one bedroom on the Lower Level. Some beds are in shared spaces rather than private rooms.

04 · Baths & spa

Four baths. One of them has a sauna.

The spa bath pairs a glass rain shower with a cedar sauna. Double vanities elsewhere keep mornings moving when twelve people need the mirror.

05 · Outside

A deck, a hot tub, a fire ring — and a whole clearing.

Outdoor dining on the deck, a hot tub facing the valley, hammocks and a stone fire ring on the lawn. Through the fieldstone wall: The Clearing, a deck in the woods for yoga, meditation and quiet acoustic evenings.

Before you book

Good to know

Getting hereAbout 3 hours and 143 road miles from Manhattan. In snow, a four- or all-wheel-drive car is strongly recommended.
Check-in & outCheck-in 4 pm – 1 am, checkout 11 am. Codes arrive the day before.
Group sizeUp to 12 overnight guests. Pricing depends on headcount, so book with your real number.
SignalNo cell service near the house. Wi-Fi inside — save your access code before you leave.
SafetyThe hot tub has no gate or lock — keep an eye on children. Exterior cameras face the entrance only.
House rulesPlease keep the fire in the ring. The full house rules are on the Airbnb listing.
