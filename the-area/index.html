By season

What to do, every season

WinterDecember – March
  • Ski Belleayre or Plattekill
  • Snowshoe Kelly Hollow
  • Sauna, then snow
  • Wood stove, board games

“Even in the wintertime it didn't feel cramped at all with 8 adults and 1 toddler”
Helena · December 2025

SpringApril – May
  • Waterfalls at full flow
  • Trout season opens April 1
  • Market opens mid-May
  • Quiet trails, fewer crowds

“The place had plenty of games for the first rainy night…”
Amy · May 2025

SummerJune – August
  • Saturdays at the Round Barn
  • Paddle the Pepacton (permit)
  • Lawn games, grill, fire ring
  • Stargazing from The Clearing

“Deer and other wild animals came to the backyard…”
Marisol · August 2023 · translated

AutumnSeptember – November
  • Foliage, usually peaking early–mid October
  • Apple picking and cider
  • Hot tub on cold nights
  • Late market through Thanksgiving

“…it was absolutely breath taking sitting in the hot tub and taking in the views.”
Paula · October 2024

Ski & ride

Two mountains, two moods.

Photo: Daniel Case, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons
BelleayreHighmount · 15 mi
62trails
1,404 ftvertical
96%snowmaking

State-run, big and well groomed: 171 skiable acres off a 3,429 ft summit, with a gondola to the top. Good for mixed-ability groups.

PlattekillRoxbury · 11 mi
41trails
1,100 ftvertical
Fri–Sun+ holidays

The last family-run mountain in the Catskills. Steeper, quieter, full of character.

Stats from belleayre.com and plattekill.com, checked Sept 2026. Confirm hours and tickets before you go.
Outdoors & towns

Woods, water, main streets.

Hike

“Forever wild,” by law.

35 peaks over 3,500 ft and 300+ miles of marked trail in the Catskill Park. Kelly Hollow’s forest loops are 18 miles away.

Photo: Shannon1, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia CommonsWater

The reservoir that waters New York.

The Pepacton, 16 miles away, is the largest reservoir in New York City’s system. Paddling and fishing need an NYC DEP permit.

Photo: Watershed Post, CC BY 2.0Towns & markets

Main streets and a round red barn.

Margaretville is 6 miles away for groceries and coffee. Saturdays from mid-May, the Pakatakan market fills the Round Barn in Halcottsville.
